# Test 6 — Decyde (decyde.co.uk), my own live product

An AI-visibility audit: it checks how a business shows up when buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for recommendations, reports what the AI says versus competitors, and recommends fixes. Positioned for Caribbean businesses selling to foreign buyers. I built it. I ran the skill on it.

VERDICT: BULLETIN

Why (worst first)
- The live product is a free one-off audit, and so is the market's. My own Product Hunt listing sells a "free AI visibility audit… results in 60 seconds" with no price and no Caribbean positioning. HubSpot's AI Search Grader does the same thing free, no account. I was competing with free, and my own listing agreed.
- The graveyard says the audit doesn't change behaviour. Lorelight, a GEO tracker, shut in 2025 — not for lack of money but because its insights "had zero impact on customer behaviour"; buyers churned once they saw the report. XFunnel was absorbed by HubSpot in Oct 2025. The value is in the fix (schema, citations, PR), which is ongoing casework. The report is the brochure for a service.
- The crowd is funded and bundling: Otterly $29/mo, Peec $95, Semrush AI Toolkit $99, Profound $99, Scrunch $250, AthenaHQ $295, Ahrefs Brand Radar $199. Google, Semrush and HubSpot are folding monitoring into tools the prospect already pays for.

What the LLM doesn't have here: live specificity (running the prompts across four engines weekly) — but it's cheap, commoditised and given away free.

Time: the cold-email pipeline as designed (~1 hour research + scan per prospect × 25 a week) is my entire weekly budget before any product work, engine drift, billing or support.

Money: does not reach the target as specified. Six paid audits a month from ~100 cold emails at a realistic close rate is half a sale.

Smallest test and kill line: withheld — I'm running it, with a date. What I will say: the skill told me to drop my first target vertical and put a price on the site.

Note: the skill could not reach decyde.co.uk through the session proxy; site evidence came from the Product Hunt listing.
